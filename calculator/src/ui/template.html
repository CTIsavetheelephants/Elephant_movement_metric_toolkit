1. Load your tracking data

A CSV or Excel export from your collar system (EarthRanger, Movebank, Africa Wildlife Tracking, Vectronic, Lotek, ...). One row per GPS fix.

Load all your files for this study area at once, covering the whole time since the collars were fitted, not just your latest download. Select or drop several files together; overlapping downloads are fine (repeated fixes are removed). If you saved the combined tracking data file from a previous run, load it together with your new download(s).

How should your data be formatted?

  • An elephant name or ID column
  • A date and time for each fix (one column, or separate date and time columns)
  • Coordinates: latitude/longitude in decimal degrees, UTM easting/northing, or a WKT "POINT (lon lat)" column
  • Optional: sex, species, collar ID, study area (you can also enter sex later)

Extra columns are fine (speed, temperature, battery, DOP, ...): they are ignored. Column names do not have to match exactly; you can check and change which column is which in the next step.

Any extra columns, such as those shaded above, are fine to leave in.

Fixes should be about 1-4 hours apart. Collars with a typical interval longer than 6 hours cannot be used for these metrics and are skipped.
